I’m drawn to complex systems. Not just making them faster, but making them safer, more stable, and easier to operate under real production pressure.

My work has centered around infrastructure, observability, automation, and reliability, with a strong bias toward practical solutions that hold up in the real world.

I’m always looking for better ways to solve hard problems, learn new systems deeply, and build technology that other engineers can depend on.
